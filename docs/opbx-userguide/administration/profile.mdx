# Profile Settings

Access your profile by clicking your name or avatar in the top-right corner, then selecting **Profile**.

## Personal Profile

The Personal Profile section contains your account information.

### Editable Fields

| Field | Editable | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Name | Yes | Display name shown throughout OPBX |
| Email | No | Contact your administrator to change email |
| Phone Number | Yes | Contact number for notifications |
| Street Address | Yes | Organization address line 1 |
| City | Yes | City name |
| State/Province | Yes | State or province |
| Postal Code | Yes | ZIP or postal code |
| Country | Yes | Country selection |

### Email Address

Your email address is tied to authentication and cannot be changed through the profile page. To change your email:

1. Contact your organization administrator
2. Or contact platform support if you are the owner

## Password Change

Change your password from the Password section. You must provide:

- Current password (for verification)
- New password (must meet complexity requirements)
- Confirm new password

:::note
After changing your password, you will be logged out of all devices and need to log in again with the new password.
:::

Password requirements:

- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character

## Organization Settings

The Organization Settings section appears only for organization owners. It contains settings that affect the entire organization.

### Organization Name

The display name for your organization. This appears in:

- The browser tab title
- Email notifications
- Platform management interfaces

### Timezone

Select your organization's primary timezone from over 65 options. Timezones are grouped by region:

| Region | Example Timezones |
|--------|-------------------|
| Americas | America/New_York, America/Los_Angeles, America/Chicago |
| Europe | Europe/London, Europe/Paris, Europe/Berlin |
| Asia | Asia/Tokyo, Asia/Singapore, Asia/Dubai |
| Africa | Africa/Johannesburg, Africa/Cairo, Africa/Lagos |
| Australia | Australia/Sydney, Australia/Melbourne, Australia/Perth |
| Pacific | Pacific/Auckland, Pacific/Fiji, Pacific/Honolulu |
| UTC | UTC, UTC+0, UTC+1 through UTC+14 |

The selected timezone affects:

- Call history timestamps
- Report generation
- Scheduled operations
- Business hours calculations

:::tip
Choose the timezone where most of your users are located. You can view call times in other timezones through reporting features.
:::
